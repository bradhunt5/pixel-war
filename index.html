    How to play
    • Pick a spot for your capital. Rival factions start elsewhere on the map.
    • Your troops grow over time. More land means more troops and a higher cap.
    • Set how many troops to send with the Send slider, then tap (or drag your finger onto) any land that borders yours.
    • Grey land is unclaimed and cheap to take. Enemy land costs more the more troops they hold.
    • Troops on the march leave home weaker, so don't overreach.
    • Wipe out every rival to win. Lose all your land and it's over.
