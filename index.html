    How to play
    • On World or Europe, tap a country to lead it. On Random, tap anywhere to place your capital.
    • Troops and gold grow over time. More land means more of both.
    • Set the Send slider, then tap land to attack. Across water, your troops sail there by boat ⛵.
    • Drag to pan and pinch to zoom.
    • 🏗️ Build spends gold: 🏙️ cities raise your troop cap, ⛏️ mines on gold deposits (yellow dots) earn gold, 🛡️ forts make nearby land 3× harder to take, 🎯 artillery shells enemies in range, and ☢️ silos let you launch nukes.
    • Tap one of your buildings to upgrade it (up to level 3): bigger nukes and faster reloads, richer mines, longer-range artillery, stronger forts and bigger cities.
    • Captured buildings change hands. Nuked land stays irradiated for a while.
    • Wipe out every rival to win. Lose all your land and it's over.
